Frequently Asked Questions

Common user queries about pricing, wallets, and payments.

The final cost of your print job consists solely of the base printing tariff (calculated by the number of pages, sides, and color mode) plus a standard 2.5% tax and service charge. There are no registration fees, platform subscription charges, or hidden order processing fees.

Color quality printing ranges from 17 PKR to 30 PKR based on the density and quality required:
  • Standard (17 PKR): Simple accents, charts, diagrams, and high-volume school presentations.
  • Enhanced (23 PKR): Better saturation for standard photos, colorful flyers, and premium projects.
  • Premium (30 PKR): Full page high-density color prints, graphics, and high-resolution photos.

No, wallet top-ups are completely free. When you add funds to your FastCopy wallet via mobile payment processors (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank transfer), 100% of the amount is credited directly to your balance. The 2.5% Tax & Service Charge is only calculated on active print orders at the time of purchase.

If a print shop has a paper jam, printer breakdown, or rejects your print file, the shop worker can cancel your order through the print queue application. The full amount paid (including the 2.5% Tax & Service Charge) is refunded instantly to your student app wallet ledger.
